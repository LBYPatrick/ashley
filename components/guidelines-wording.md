# Documentation and Messages to the User

Use ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English for documentation and all AI messages to the user. This includes progress reports, explanations, questions, and completion reports.

- Apply the writing rules and approved dictionary meanings in the [official ASD-STE100 standard](https://www.asd-ste100.org/).
- Use approved words with their approved meanings and parts of speech. Use technical nouns and technical verbs when the subject requires them.
- Use the same technical term for the same item. Explain an unfamiliar term at its first use.
- Use short, complete sentences. Keep necessary articles and other words. Do not use contractions.
- Use the active voice for instructions. Use the imperative form and give one instruction in each sentence.
- Keep procedural sentences to 20 words or fewer. Keep descriptive sentences to 25 words or fewer.
- Give each paragraph one subject. Use lists for procedures and complex information.
- Remove idioms, figurative language, and unnecessary words. Keep all facts, conditions, limits, and safety information.
- Keep code, commands, identifiers, paths, API names, and quoted source text exact. Apply these wording rules to the explanation around them.
- Check the wording before delivery. Do not claim full ASD-STE100 compliance unless you checked the applicable rules and dictionary entries.
